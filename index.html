Mastering the Embed

A step-by-step guide to adding video content to your website.

Step 1: Locate the Share Button

Navigate to the YouTube video you wish to use. Look directly beneath the video player and click on the "Share" button located next to the Like/Dislike options.

Step 2: Select the Embed Option

A pop-up menu will appear with various social sharing icons. Click on the first icon labeled "Embed" (indicated by the <> brackets).

Step 3: Copy the iFrame Code

A panel will open showing the HTML code on the right. You can toggle options like "Show player controls." Once ready, click the "Copy" button in the bottom right corner.

Step 4: Paste into Your HTML

Open your website's HTML file in your code editor. Paste the copied code (the <iframe> tag) into the section of the body where you want the video to appear.
